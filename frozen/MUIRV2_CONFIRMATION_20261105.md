# Muir-V2 confirmation protocol (5 November 2026)

Decision: use the previously sealed 901 counterpart pairs, disjoint from the 399-pair exploratory first pass, for one final confirmation of the pre-existing Muir-V2 adaptation. The user delegated the decision on whether to open this set on 5 November 2026. Selection of this confirmation question and method contrasts was informed by first-pass results; this is a newly locked confirmation on independent source pairs, not a claim of a protocol written before the first pass. The original split, seven adaptation rules, prompts, checkpoints and 320-token decoding budget remain unchanged. This protocol concerns Muir-V2 validity only, not native answering or the other public benchmarks.

## Population and procedures

Use all 901 IDs in `MUIR_SPLIT_20261022.json` and all seven Muir-V2 variants per pair (6,307 items), verifying the pinned source hash, exactly seven distinct instances per pair, unique item IDs, complete split coverage and disjointness from first pass. Each image's published-answer-derived label is inherited, not human-adjudicated. Do not use these pairs for training, calibration, method development, or checkpoint or threshold selection. Run all ten original first-pass validity methods: Qwen3-VL-2B, Qwen3-VL-8B, OmniVerifier-7B common and native-format prompts, and seeds 0–2 of structured and describe verifiers. Use the original common-prompt wording, including its Korean language tag, and the original Omni native prompt. No first-pass predictions are merged with these 901-pair results. Greedy generation and parsing follow the existing implementation; missing/unparseable verdicts count as not flagged. Record exact model snapshots, adapters, source/prompt implementation hashes and resumable per-item outputs.

## Analysis fixed before confirmation predictions

Primary endpoint: invalid-versus-valid balanced accuracy (BA). Co-primary comparisons: (1) mean BA of three structured checkpoints minus the zero-shot 2B BA; (2) OmniVerifier common-prompt BA minus mean BA of the three structured checkpoints. Report both signed contrasts, source-pair clustered bootstrap 97.5% percentile intervals using 10,000 paired resamples, RNG seed 20261105, and all per-checkpoint values. The two wider intervals provide a Bonferroni familywise 95% coverage target, subject to bootstrap assumptions. The checkpoints are fixed; these intervals do not estimate training-seed variance. Do not claim one contrast confirms the other, and do not treat a nonsignificant result as equivalence.

Also report per-method BA, accuracy, invalid recall, invalid precision, valid retention, parse-failure counts and per-instance-type rates; report the fixed always-invalid, None-present and key-is-None heuristics. Report native Muir-V2 class proportions and exact coverage. Previously frozen dev thresholds may be reported separately without retuning; test-selected thresholds are descriptive only. No unseen significance test, winner-selected checkpoint, supplementary seed or modified prompt may be promoted to a primary result. If some jobs fail, the report must show missing counts and withhold primary contrasts until the specified comparisons are complete.

This is a confirmation on the remaining pairs of the same public benchmark and adaptation, not confirmation on independently collected images or a guarantee against foundation-model pretraining exposure. Prompt/domain mismatch, answer-key assumptions, potential text shortcuts, adaptation semantics, dependent variants, seed variability and any incomplete model outputs remain explicit limitations. Negative and mixed outcomes will be reported.
